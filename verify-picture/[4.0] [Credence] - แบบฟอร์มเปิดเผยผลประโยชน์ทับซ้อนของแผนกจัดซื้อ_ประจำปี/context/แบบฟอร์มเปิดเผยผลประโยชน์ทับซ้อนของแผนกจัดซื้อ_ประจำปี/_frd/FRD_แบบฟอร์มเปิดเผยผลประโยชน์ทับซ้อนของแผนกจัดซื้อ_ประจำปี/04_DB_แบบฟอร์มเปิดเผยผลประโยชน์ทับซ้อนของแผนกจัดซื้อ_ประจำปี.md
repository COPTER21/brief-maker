## §4.3 Indexes / Constraints
- unique (tenant_id, doc_no) · index (tenant_id, declarant_id, disclosure_year, status) · check (has_conflict <> 'has' OR status = 'draft' OR length(trim(conflict_detail)) ≥ detail_min) — server enforce (FN-01)
- row policy: SELECT = role-compliance ∨ declarant_id = me ∨ (status ≠ 'draft' ∧ me ∈ approval_chain[].assignee)
