## §4.3 T_agm_checkin_sync
id, checkin_id_fk, cbm_code, event('CHECK_IN'), to_status('รอผลการเท'), idempotency_key(=checkin_id), attempt, result(success/failed), old_status, new_status, version, error, trace_id, at · **UNIQUE(idempotency_key)** (idempotent)

## §4.4 T_agm_checkin_audit (append-only)
id, checkin_id_fk, action(create/sync/retry/void), actor_id, at, reason?, details · REVOKE UPDATE/DELETE

## §4.5 Relationships
```
T_cbm_bookings ──dispatching──▶ T_agm_checkins (pending, cbm_code)
T_agm_checkins ──CHECK_IN event──▶ T_cbm_bookings (booking → รอผลการเท) via T_agm_checkin_sync
T_quota ──read──▶ T_agm_checkins (member)
```
