## §4.6 Data Classification
### Levels
| Level | Fields | UI | Export |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restricted | citizen_id, history diff | mask list · เต็ม detail+ACL+audit | excluded (S07-01) |
| Confidential | phone/email/address×2/bank/BAAC/beneficiary/line_id/documents | ตาม role | excluded ถ้าไม่ผ่าน |
| Internal (default) | ที่เหลือ | แสดง | ตาม role |
### PII overlay
PII = ชื่อ, citizen_id, phone, email, address×2, beneficiary, line_id, bank accounts → PDPA scope · citizen_id = PII+Restricted
### Enforcement
citizen_id: encrypt at rest + mask list + ACL detail · bank/BAAC: mask ตาม permission · documents: Document Access (S08-02) + audit
### Policy Center linkage
citizen_id → Restricted Resources (OQ-F02) · ENC matrix (§4) · documents → Document Access registry
