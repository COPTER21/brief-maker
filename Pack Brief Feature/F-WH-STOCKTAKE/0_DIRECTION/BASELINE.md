# ERP baseline — Stocktake · 2026-09-14

[Odoo physical inventory](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/count_products.html) distinguishes counted from on-hand, plans full counts and warns of movement between count and adjustment; [Odoo cycle counts](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/cycle_counts.html) covers recurring location-based counts. CUBE Stocktake locks a scope-specific freeze/snapshot, assigns blind sheets, requires threshold recount and DOA, then sends a mock request to separate Stock Adjustment. This is a stricter separation than applying quantity directly. [ASSUMED] no movement outside selected scope is frozen; supervisor alone may reveal system balance.
