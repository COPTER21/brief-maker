Quantities use DECIMAL(18,6) with tenant UoM normalization; rounding to Item base UoM/precision is `[ASSUMED]` owner Item/UoM. Effective day is tenant-local `[ASSUMED]`, store UTC instant plus local date/zone. `createdAt/by`, `correlationId`, `expectedVersion` audit all policy mutations. Old policy/event rows are never deleted or updated by ROP. Retention/security owner decides archive; no silent purge.

## Classification enforcement §4.2/4.6
Public: none in stock quantities. Internal: item/warehouse display names. Confidential: policy values, stock snapshot, payload and ack. Restricted: actor identifiers; PII flag true. DB grants, API authorization, UI redaction and audit export apply according to class; Policy Center owns overrides/Restricted Resources registration. Client must not receive other tenant rows. Audit logs avoid raw personal names or NC recipients.

## §4.6 Four-level enforcement
Public: no ROP business column. Internal: operational codes/refs/status, scoped by tenant/warehouse. Confidential: thresholds, stock, payload/ack/hash, returned only to authorized operations; redact from routine logs. Restricted: actor references, returned only to auditor/authorized reviewer and registered with Policy Center Restricted Resources after owner confirmation. PII flag is independent of class. API rejects cross-tenant queries; UI hides actor from general users; audit export preserves trace with controls. `AvailabilitySnapshot` is upstream, not a ROP table: item/warehouse/asOf Internal, onHand/held Confidential, snapshotRef Internal, no PII.
