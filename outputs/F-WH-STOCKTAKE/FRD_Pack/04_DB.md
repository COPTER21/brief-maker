## §4.3 Constraints and Indexes

- unique active `(tenant_id,scope_key)` พร้อม overlap check ใน transaction
- unique `(round_id,item_id,location_id)` snapshot
- unique `(round_id,count_round)` count sheet และ count_round ∈ {1,2}
- `counted_qty >= 0`; submitted sheet ต้องครบ snapshot count
- unique `(round_id,level)` approval step; decision append/update guarded by version
- unique `(round_id)` handoff, unique idempotency key within tenant
- indexes: round tenant/status, lock tenant/active, sheet assignee/status, event round/seq
- RLS ทุก table ด้วย tenant_id

## §4.4 Relationships

Round 1:N snapshot, sheets, approval steps, audit; sheet 1:N lines; round 1:0..1 lock and handoff. Item/Location/User/DOA/F082 เป็น soft reference ข้าม feature

## §4.6 Enforcement

Internal: tenant auth; Confidential: role projection/mask + audit view/export. PII overlay applies to person ids/names. Policy Center must register confidential stock/approval fields before production. Retention: round/count/approval/handoff/audit 7 years or Warehouse policy, whichever longer
