## P-01 Policy drawer

Searchable pickers: Item, Warehouse, optional Preferred Vendor. Fields: Min, Max, Safety, Lead Time, ADU Window 30/60/90, Pack Size, Effective Date. Validation appears below the failing field with red border and `aria-invalid=true`. Save closes drawer and shows toast only after success.

## P-02 Suggestion drawer

Display ATP from F009, ROP, human-readable reason, On-Order, ADU/Lead Time, target Max+Safety, raw/Pack Size, suggested qty and status. Success of Notification/PR uses toast; drawer result area is reserved for actionable errors.

## Empty/error/loading

- No policy: card separated from pickers, names selected pair and offers “เพิ่มนโยบาย”.
- No snapshot: explicit unavailable card; no side-effect action.
- Busy: disable trigger button and show `กำลัง…`.
- Replay: toast states result already exists; no duplicate history row.

## Overlay and dismissal

Drawer is 920px/max 100vw. Picker menu portals to `#overlay-root` above drawer and flips near viewport edge. Esc closes picker before drawer; closing drawer removes portal menus. Backdrop closes drawer. Toast is top z-layer.

## Demo-only

`.demo-only` is prototype identity only and must be absent/hidden in production; it is not a role switch or business capability.
