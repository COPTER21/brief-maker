No layout instruction here overrides the approved HTML. Use its design tokens and responsive behavior as source of truth.

## 1.1 P-01 Credit Limit List
- Search retains focus/caret while filtering; filters: central status and risk/utilization; sortable columns include code/name/limit/utilization.
- Each row opens P-02. Cash customers show `เงินสด`; no credit bar/AR details.
- Required states: populated, empty result, near-full, over-limit, hold, draft, pending approval.

## 1.2 P-02 Credit Profile Detail Drawer
- Header shows customer, current credit state, available limit and actions `ปรับวงเงิน`, hold/unhold, review.
- Tabs are profile summary, AR transactions/a ging/DSO, audit, and `สายอนุมัติ (DOA)`.
- Approval timeline preserves status dot/check and shows avatar, approver name, position, time, and current step. It must render line segments between dots, never through a dot.
- Footer actions are state driven: draft=`ส่งอนุมัติ`; pending=current approver may approve/reject; completed has no mutation action.

## 1.3 P-03 Change Drawer
Fields: current limit read-only, requested limit, reason, derived tier/DOA preview. `บันทึกคำขอ` creates draft. `ส่งอนุมัติ` is visually disabled until amount differs, reason exists, and a DOA tier resolves.

## 1.4 P-04 Reason Confirmations
Hold, unhold and reject require reason. Esc follows modal → change drawer → detail drawer → close.
